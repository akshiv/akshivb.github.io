About Me

Home About

I'm a software engineer who spends a lot of time thinking about hardware. Originally from New Delhi, I grew up in Vancouver, where I studied at the University of British Columbia, graduating with a Bachelor of Applied Science in Engineering Physics and a Minor in Honors Mathematics. Currently, I'm working in the Bay as a software engineer, mostly at hardware companies.

In my spare time you'll find me out hiking / biking / running, cooking, trying exotic coffees, or tinkering on some small hardware project I enjoy learning new things! I'm an energy and public policy nerd, so if you want to chat about urban infill, fast breeder reactors, or learn what either of those mean, I'm your guy.

Below is my resume if you want a structured look at what I've been up to. I'm not actively looking but feel free to reach out if you think you have something that might interest me.
